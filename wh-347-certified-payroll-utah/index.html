WH-347 searches usually mean the contractor has an urgent project problem.

A Utah contractor may not be looking for payroll software in general. They may be trying to figure out how to produce weekly certified payroll records for federal Davis Bacon or related funded project requirements. Utah does not have a broad state prevailing wage law, but federal and funded project rules can still matter.

What WH-347 usually asks payroll to prove

  • Employee and identifying payroll details
  • Work classification or role on the project
  • Hours worked and project week details
  • Rate, deductions, and net wages
  • A signed certification tied to the payroll records
