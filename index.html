What ZayZoon is

ZayZoon is an employee financial wellness benefit that lets workers access part of their earned pay before payday. It is free to the employer, funds the early payout, and reconciles automatically through payroll, so employers are not manually fronting advances. Through Auris, it becomes one more benefit you can offer employees without rerouting paychecks or creating another payroll headache.

What Earned Employee Wages means

Earned Employee Wages are wages an employee has already worked for, but has not received yet because payday has not arrived. With earned wage access, employees can request a limited portion of those wages early when life happens. ZayZoon lists safeguards like one transaction per day, up to 50% of earned wages, and a $200 daily max.
