ZayZoon quick scan

What is ZayZoon?

ZayZoon is an earned wage access benefit. It can let employees access part of the wages they have already earned before payday, without the employer fronting payroll advances.

No cost to employers. Low cost option for employees, commonly $5 when they choose an instant payout.
No more advances

Stop fronting money manually

Instead of owners or managers handling one off payroll advances, employees can use ZayZoon to access earned wages through a structured benefit.

No employer cost

Simple to offer

ZayZoon can be offered at no cost to the employer, which makes it easier to add as an employee financial wellness benefit.

Low employee cost

Clear employee choice

Employees choose whether to use it. Instant payouts are commonly a low flat cost, often $5, instead of a payday loan style experience.

Why employers care

Financial stress shows up at work. Earned wage access can give employees a safer option when timing is the problem, while helping the employer avoid manual advance requests.

How Auris makes this easier. ZayZoon can fit into the broader Auris payroll review, so earned wage access is not another disconnected vendor for the employer to manage alone.

WOTC quick scan

Auris scans for tax credits.

WOTC screening is a standard feature that helps identify whether new hires may qualify for federal hiring tax credits.

The Work Opportunity Tax Credit rewards employers for hiring people from certain groups that face barriers to employment.

Hire an eligible employee, receive a potential federal tax credit.
Up to $9,600

Disabled veteran

Veteran with a service connected disability and unemployed for at least 6 months before being hired.

Up to $9,000

Long term family assistance recipient

Individual who has received long term TANF benefits.

Up to $5,600

Veteran

Veteran unemployed for at least 6 months before being hired.

Up to $4,800

Disabled veteran

Veteran with a service connected disability hired within 1 year of discharge.

Up to $2,400

Common qualifying categories

  • Veteran receiving SNAP
  • Veteran unemployed at least 4 weeks but less than 6 months
  • SNAP recipient
  • SSI recipient
  • TANF recipient
  • Qualified ex felon
  • Vocational rehabilitation referral
  • Designated community resident
  • Long term unemployment recipient, 27 or more consecutive weeks
Up to $1,200

Summer youth employee

Youth employee working in a designated Empowerment Zone during the summer.

Why employers care

Multiple eligible employees can generate multiple credits.

  • 2 SNAP recipients can mean up to $4,800
  • 1 veteran unemployed 6 months or more can mean up to $5,600
  • Potential tax credits in this example: $10,400
Most payroll systems do not screen for this. Some that do may charge a percentage. Auris can screen automatically and uses a flat $299 fee after screening instead of a percentage model.

Timing matters. If WOTC authorization is pending renewal, employers still need to capture screening information on time. Historically, when WOTC has been renewed after a lapse, it has often been made retroactive, but the filing window is still tight. The key deadline is generally 28 days from hire or from the retroactive application window when guidance allows it.
